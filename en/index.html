About REMAL

A modern house for fragrance distribution.

REMAL is a Libyan company dedicated to the import and distribution of fine fragrances. We represent perfume houses in the Libyan market and place their collections with the right distributors, perfumeries and points of sale in every major city.

Like sand carried across the desert, we move fragrance from where it is made to where it is loved — with care for the brand, its image and its customer.
